How Ajo works on AJO

Money moves between members and the admin as usual. AJO keeps the record honest.

1. Create your team

Set the amount, members and payout order, then share one invite link.

2. Members transfer

Everyone pays into the admin's bank account, then submits their reference.

3. Admin confirms

Confirmed payments build the Ajo Balance. The month's recipient gets paid.

Everything your group needs

Contribution tracking

See paid and unpaid members at a glance, month by month.

Monthly payout schedule

A clear timeline of who collects and when.

Full transparency

Members see the balance, the recipient and their own history.

Installs like an app

Add AJO to your phone's home screen from the browser.

AJO never holds your money

There is no wallet and no bank link. The Ajo Balance is the amount the admin has confirmed, minus payouts the admin has recorded. It is a record, not a bank balance.

Questions

Does AJO collect payments?

No. Members pay the admin directly. The admin confirms each payment in the app.

How do members join?

Through the admin's invite link. Joining always makes you a member, never an admin.

Can I run more than one group?

Yes. Every team has its own members, schedule and history.
